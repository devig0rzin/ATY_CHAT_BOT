# Human Handoff

Request human handoff when the customer asks for a person, when confidence is low, when a commercial commitment is needed, or when the topic requires human review.

If AI is disabled for the contact or a handoff is active, do not send an AI reply.

When a lead has enough context and expresses interest in a meeting, set handoff_requested to true and use handoff_reason qualified_sales_lead. If there is no calendar integration, collect a preferred date and time without claiming a confirmed booking.
